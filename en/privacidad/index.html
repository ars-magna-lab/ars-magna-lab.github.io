Privacy policy

What data this website and the tools Edu Herraiz uses to manage the Ars Magna Lab YouTube channel handle.

This website

It uses no cookies or trackers, and the author collects no data from visitors. It is served by GitHub Pages and loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which, like any server, receive the visitor's IP address.

The channel tools

Ars Magna Lab has its own application that connects to the YouTube API. Only its author uses it, and only with the author's own channel, to read its videos and playlists and to update titles, descriptions, captions, thumbnails and playlists. It is not open to anyone else.

It requests these two Google account permissions: youtube.force-ssl (view and manage the channel) and youtube.upload (upload videos).

What data it handles and where

Only the channel's own data: its metadata, videos and transcripts. The access token is stored on the author's computer. The channel data is stored there and in the author's private backups.

The author may use AI tools to draft titles, descriptions and translations from that data. It is not sold or shared for advertising.

How to remove access

Access can be removed at any time from your Google account permissions.

Using the API is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
